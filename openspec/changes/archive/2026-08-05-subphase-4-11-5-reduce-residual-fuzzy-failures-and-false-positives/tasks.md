## 6. Hybrid Guard

- [ ] 6.1 Add a narrowly-scoped guard in `_decision` (or its equivalent in `_prediction` / `_hybrid_prediction`) at `backend/services/product_recognition_calibration_runner.py`. The guard fires only when `case.get("catalog_scope") == "pending_product_selection_restricted"` AND the fuzzy decision is explicitly `ambiguous` (the equivalent direct evidence being `len(fuzzy_ids) > 1` on `CaseObservation.fuzzy_ids`, OR a category-level `encontrados_posibles` group with `kind: "category"` detected by `_fuzzy_decision(result)`). The fuzzy decision is threaded into `_decision` as a derived parameter from `observation.fuzzy_ids` and the category discriminator (computed once in `_hybrid_prediction`, e.g. `fuzzy_decision = _fuzzy_decision(fuzzy_result)`) and passed alongside the combined hybrid `ranking` / `scores`. The guard MUST inspect the fuzzy decision explicitly and MUST NOT infer fuzzy ambiguity from the combined hybrid `ranking` (the previous `len(ranking) > 1` proxy is unsafe because the combined hybrid ranking is the union of `observation.fuzzy_ids` and `observation.vector_ids`, so a fuzzy-`unique` + vector-`unique(other)` case produces a multi-candidate hybrid ranking even though the fuzzy was not ambiguous). The guard returns `"ambiguous"` immediately, before the scoring rule consults `policy.fuzzy_weight`, `policy.vector_weight`, `policy.unique_threshold`, `policy.ambiguous_threshold`, `policy.minimum_score_gap`, `policy.vector_top_k`, the canonical/alias promotion in `_exact_flags`, the vector's top-1, or the score combination. The guard is a single positional tag; it does not modify `_hybrid_prediction`'s scoring formula or any other calibrated surface. The guard does NOT fire when the fuzzy returns `unique` (even if the combined hybrid ranking contains multiple candidates because the vector contributed an extra candidate), does NOT fire when the fuzzy returns `unknown`, and does NOT fire for `commerce_dynamic_database` cases (the `catalog_scope` check excludes them; case origin is irrelevant to the guard — `in_memory` is NOT a disabling condition, and `commerce_dynamic_database` is NOT a disabling condition by origin alone; cases of either origin remain unaffected unless both guard conditions are satisfied).
